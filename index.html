# Privacy Policy — 2048 Infinity **Last updated: October 16, 2026** ## Introduction 2048 Infinity is a number puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it. ## Information Collection The current version of 2048 Infinity does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to play the game. Game progress, statistics, preferences, and other gameplay information may be stored locally on your device to support game functionality. ## Data Sharing The current version does not include advertising or analytics services that transmit gameplay information to third parties. The developer does not collect or receive gameplay information through the app. ## Internet Access The core game is designed to work offline. Features that require external services, if introduced in future versions, may be subject to an updated Privacy Policy. ## Children's Privacy The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If future versions introduce services that collect information, this policy will be updated accordingly. ## Data Security and Retention Gameplay information stored locally remains subject to your device's storage and settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove locally stored information. ## Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the privacy policy webpage. ## Contact For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer through the contact details provided on the app's Google Play listing.
